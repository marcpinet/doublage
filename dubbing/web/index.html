Calibrate your audio delay

Wireless (Bluetooth) headsets add a recording delay. Put your headphones on. The circle below shows the rhythm: on every flash you'll make a short audible sound into your mic — say "BAH!", snap, or tap the mic, right on the beat. When you've got the rhythm, press Go: a countdown starts, then make your sound on each flash. We measure your delay and compensate automatically.
Watch the rhythm…

Set up your audio delay

For your dub to land in sync, calibrate your microphone delay — especially with Bluetooth headphones (they add 150–300 ms). It takes ~15 seconds and is remembered on this browser. You can also do it later from the lobby.
